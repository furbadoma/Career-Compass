🌟 7-Day Career Challenge

Explore your interests through one small activity each day.

Day 1 — Discover

Think about what you enjoy doing and write down three activities that make you curious or excited.

Day 2 — Explore

Find one career or profession that interests you. Learn what people in this career actually do.

Day 3 — Try

Spend 20–30 minutes trying a small activity related to your interests.

Day 4 — Learn

Watch a short video, read an article, or explore a beginner resource connected to the direction you are interested in.

Day 5 — Create

Create something small connected to your interests: an idea, design, plan, presentation, or project.

Day 6 — Reflect

Write down what you enjoyed, what was difficult, and what you would like to explore more.

Day 7 — Next Step

Choose one small thing you can do next to continue exploring this career direction.
